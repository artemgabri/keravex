Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Keravex ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit keravex.com or reserve a founding spot.

Information we collect

When you reserve a founding spot or join the waitlist, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Name and shipping address (only if you complete a paid reservation through Gumroad)
  • Payment information (processed by Gumroad and its payment partners — we never see or store your card details)
  • Browsing data through cookies, Meta Pixel, and analytics (see Cookies section below)

How we use your information

  • To process your reservation and send order confirmations
  • To send you product updates, launch announcements, and educational content related to GLP-1 nutrition
  • To improve our website and marketing
  • To comply with legal obligations

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email, or by writing to hi@keravex.com.

Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies including Meta Pixel (Facebook), Google Analytics, and other marketing pixels to understand how visitors use our site and to deliver relevant advertising. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with:

  • Gumroad — our payment and order processor
  • Email service providers — to deliver transactional and marketing emails
  • Advertising partners (Meta, Google) — for analytics and remarketing, in aggregated or hashed form
  • Legal authorities — when required by law

Refund policy

Your $20 founding-spot reservation is fully refundable on demand within 30 days. To request a refund, email hi@keravex.com with your order ID. Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days through Gumroad to your original payment method.

If we are unable to ship by October 31, 2026, your reservation will be automatically refunded in full with no action required from you, in accordance with the FTC Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule (16 CFR Part 435).

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area or California, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, and to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise these rights, email hi@keravex.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion at any time.

Children

Our products are not directed at individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Material changes will be communicated via email to active customers.
